Why a robotaxi fleet works

Remove the driver and a taxi stops being a job and becomes an asset. Each vehicle earns around the clock while costs stay flat and predictable.

Earns while others sleep

No shifts, no driver fatigue. Vehicles serve late-night airport runs and early commutes — the hours human-driven fleets leave on the table.

Pennies per mile

Off-peak depot charging costs a fraction of gasoline, and EV drivetrains cut maintenance to tires and wipers. Energy runs about 5–6 cents per mile.

Built to scale

Fleet I is 10 vehicles on one depot. The playbook — vehicles, charging, cleaning, insurance, dispatch — repeats city by city as regulation opens.

Fleet I: ten Tesla robotaxis

Robotaxi-spec Teslas running unsupervised Full Self-Driving, operated from a single secured depot in Orange County.

  • Vehicle platform. Tesla Model Y, robotaxi configuration with FSD — the same platform Tesla operates in its own robotaxi network.
  • Depot operations. Overnight Level 2 charging at off-peak rates, daily interior turnover cleaning, and scheduled deep details.
  • Fleet care. Commercial autonomous-fleet insurance, telematics on every vehicle, and a two-person operations team for the whole fleet.
  • Uptime target. 97% — vehicles rotate through charging and cleaning so the fleet never sleeps all at once.
